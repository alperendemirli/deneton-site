One loop, from the invitation to the close-out.

  1. Invite

    Send an invitation when an item is ready for inspection. The people who need to attend are notified.

  2. Witness

    Record the outcome, the progress and the photos on the spot. Unplanned inspections can be recorded without an invitation.

  3. Track

    The tracker shows every asset against every stage, so open, failed and upcoming work stays in view.

  4. Close out

    Raise snags against what failed, assign them to a person or a company, and follow them until they are closed.

Built around how inspections run on site.

Your stages and your fields

Set up the inspection stages, classifications and outcomes each project uses. Nothing is tied to one discipline or one type of contract.

Records you can stand behind

A published record is locked. A correction is a new record that supersedes the old one, and the history stays.

Sized for large projects

Import assets from Excel and work with tens of thousands of items without the tracker slowing down.

Access that follows the job

Roles are defined per project. The project owner decides who records, who configures and who only views, whichever company they work for.
